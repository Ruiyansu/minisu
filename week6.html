Week 6 Notes

Week 6 Understanding AI Algorithms

This week in our Algorithms session, we explored AI recommendation algorithms and tagging systems used on content platforms. We compared what data is accessible to regular visitors versus content creators. As a normal visitor browsing the platform, you can only view public information, such as the number of likes, saves, comments, a creator’s follower count and how many videos they have posted. More in‑depth backend data remains hidden. In contrast, creators are granted access to far more detailed analytics. These include total views, watch metrics, peak viewing times, engagement statistics and feedback from users who select "not interested", alongside many other performance indicators. I also looked into how platform tags are assigned by examining my own account. Most of my posts are costume transitions and educational introductions about traditional clothing, which are not related to anime content at all. However, because I created makeup looks inspired by characters from classic stories, the AI mistakenly labelled my account under the "anime" category. I tried to work out possible ways to correct this incorrect tagging. Platform algorithms do not always deliver accurate results for viewers either. The content pushed to users by AI may not actually match what they truly want to watch, and recommendation errors are common.
