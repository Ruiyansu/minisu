Week 1 Notes

Core Topic: Is technology making us stupid?

My personal reflection: AI can make people lazier and weaken our critical‑thinking skills if we rely on it too much. However, these negative outcomes are not inevitable. They largely depend on how individuals use the tools and broader social contexts.

Key Theory: Social Shaping

From this perspective, technology is neither totally destructive nor entirely beneficial. Technology and society complement and shape each other. Technology is capable of improving society, yet it may also cause problems such as human laziness. Biased statements like “AI will definitely ruin humanity” cannot be treated as proven facts. The actual impacts come from interactions between technology, users and social surroundings, instead of being decided by technology itself.
